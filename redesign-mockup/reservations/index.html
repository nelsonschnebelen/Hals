Valet parking, step by step

Valet directs you to the AC Marriott parking deck. Arrive by either approach below.

Alley entrance

Between Hal's and The Joseph.

  1. Turn into the alley between Hal's and The Joseph Hotel.
  2. Continue straight, then turn right onto Peabody Street.
  3. Turn right onto Rep. John Lewis Way.
  4. Turn right into the AC Marriott parking deck.

Rep. John Lewis Way entrance

From Korean Veterans Blvd.

  1. Turn onto Rep. John Lewis Way — Calacas will be on the corner.
  2. Continue past Calacas and turn into the AC Marriott parking deck.

From Peabody St., turn into the AC Marriott parking deck before reaching Calacas.

Before you visit

The house rules, briefly.

Reservations

Book on OpenTable, or call the room. Large parties should call. Nashville events use a separate request form.

The check

Hal's does not offer separate checks, except for equal payments.

Wine

Outside wine is not permitted. The list is the house list.

Dress

Business casual in both dining rooms. In Buckhead, collared shirts are expected. Beach flip-flops and slides, baseball caps, beanies, T-shirts, sweatpants and joggers, baggy pants, shorts with sneakers, sweatshirts and hoodies, sport leggings, athletic wear, and overly revealing or extremely short clothing are not allowed.

Before you book.

How do I reserve a table?

Book on OpenTable, or call the room. Large parties should call.

What are the hours?

Atlanta: Monday–Saturday 5–11 PM, Sunday 4–9 PM. The bar opens at 4 PM.

Nashville kitchen: Tuesday–Thursday 4:30–10 PM, Friday–Saturday 4:30–11 PM, Sunday–Monday 4:30–9 PM. Nashville bar: Tuesday–Thursday 4–11 PM, Friday–Saturday 4 PM–midnight, Sunday–Monday 4–10 PM.

Where do I park?

Valet is available at both houses. In Buckhead it is $5. In Nashville it is $12 at the AC Hotel Marriott parking deck on Rep. John Lewis Way S.

Is there a dress code?
