How a night is built

Tell us the occasion. We set the table.

Hal's offers private rooms and flexible seating for business meetings, family gatherings, and celebrations. A dedicated events coordinator follows the party from the first call to the last pour.

Menus can be prix-fixe or family style, with hand-cut steaks, a raw bar, shareable starters, and sides for the middle of the table. Dietary needs are handled with the kitchen.

  • Dedicated event coordinator
  • Custom printed menus and seating, on request
  • Wine pairings and a full bar
  • AV for toasts, slideshows, and presentations

Questions

Booking a private room.

What private rooms does Nashville have?

Four rooms: the Conservatory seats 6–14, the Library seats 8–16, the Study seats 12–24, and the 2nd Floor seats 24–64. A coordinator handles the room, the menu, the wine, and the timing.

Request a date through the Nashville Tripleseat form, call (615) 560-7733, or email NashvilleEvents@hals.net. The house is at 407 Korean Veterans Blvd.

How do Atlanta private events work?

Atlanta offers private rooms and flexible seating, from weekday luncheons for small groups to large parties. For a large party, contact the coordinator directly: (404) 261-0025 or EventCoordinator@hals.net.
