Our story

A Buckhead room, kept.

Hal's "The Steakhouse" opened in Atlanta in 1989. The second house opened in Nashville in 2024.

The menu, a loyal local clientele, and a relaxed charm have been a centerpiece of Buckhead for more than 35 years. Steaks, fish and shellfish, pastas, soups, and salads — served by a professional team that has been together for more than 35 years. Much of that staff has been here twenty years or longer.

It is a New Orleans-inspired menu set inside an evening: live music, the aromas of an open kitchen, a social bar, and, in Buckhead, an upper patio with the city lights. The wine list is long. Outside wine is not permitted.

Nashville carries the same cuts and the same hospitality, on the bottom floor of the AC Hotel at 407 Korean Veterans Boulevard, a few steps from Broadway.

What each city actually is.

Atlanta · Buckhead

Old Ivy

Downstairs, live music plays and the open kitchen hums. Upstairs, the patio opens onto Buckhead. It is the room Atlanta has used for anniversaries, for closing a deal, and for a Tuesday that deserved better.
