Our story

Thirty-seven years at the same table.

Hal's opened on Old Ivy in Buckhead in 1989. The idea was a steakhouse with a New Orleans streak: generous, unhurried, and built around an evening rather than a transaction. In 2024 that room came to Nashville.

A professional team that has been together for more than 35 years still runs service, and much of the staff has been here twenty years or longer. Prime steaks, fresh fish and shellfish, and nightly live music.

Private dining

Your celebration, our room.

Rehearsal dinners, milestone birthdays, the meeting that needed a closed door. Hal's hosts private and semi-private parties, with a coordinator and a menu built around the night.

In Nashville there are four rooms, from an intimate table for six to a full floor for sixty-four. Atlanta hosts weekday luncheons through large parties — call the coordinator for the room.
